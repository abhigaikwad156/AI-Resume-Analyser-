About This Project

This is an AI-powered Resume Analyzer app designed by B.Tech Students as a collage Project to help students and job seekers improve their resumes through intelligent feedback. The system uses natural language processing to evaluate resume content, extract key skills, and provide suggestions aligned with industry standards. It also generates an ATS score to estimate how well a resume performs in real recruitment systems. The goal of this project is to make resume evaluation simple, fast, and accessible, helping users increase their chances of selection in competitive job markets.
